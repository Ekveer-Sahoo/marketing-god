## Core principle
Creative is the targeting. Meta's delivery system (creators call the current one "Andromeda") matches ads to people from the creative
itself, so the main lever is **how many genuinely different creatives** you feed it, and whether you ask it for the right outcome.

## 1. Ask for the outcome you want (@marketingwithbhushan)
"Meta brings what you ask for." Traffic objective → clickers. Sales → buyers. Leads → form fillers.
2,000 clicks at ₹3 with zero sales is an objective problem, not an ad problem. Traffic campaigns are "the worst traffic you can buy".

## 2. The ad = hook + meat + CTA, built as a matrix (@hormozi)
Don't write one ad. Write **50 hooks × 3-5 meats × 1-3 CTAs = 150-750 ads a week**, film in one session, edit combinations.
- Hooks: from your winners, your free content, others' winning ads, others' free content, ad libraries. Cover the awareness levels.
- Meat (educates on problem / solution / product / offer), five formats: demonstration, testimonial, educational, story, faceless.
- CTA: show and tell exactly what to do next.

## 3. Creative diversity means different *formats*, not colours (@scalewithpb)
"Your ads aren't boring. They're all the same ad." Ten creatives with the same layout, product shot and "Shop now" are one creative; a new
background colour is not a new test, a new format is. Exercise: rebuild your best ad as a Notes-app screenshot, keep the message, run both.

### Static formats that don't look like ads (merged swipe list)
Why they work: native UI is familiar and reads as content; ugliness breaks the pattern of polished ads; negativity and gossip get read.
| Family | Formats |
|---|---|
| Native UI | Reminder notification · ChatGPT response · Google search · iPhone Notes ("breakup letter") · Instagram story reply · WhatsApp text · leaked chat · email screenshot · Facebook post text · Reddit-style post |
| Fake editorial | News headline / breaking news · bad-news ad · science article · stat headline |
| Review / proof | Zero-star review (reads as bad, is good) · testimonial grid · receipt · "what I ordered vs what I got" |
| Ugly on purpose | Badly made ad (white background, ugly fonts) · MS Paint screenshot · doodle · whiteboard · handwritten founder note · FOUND / missing poster |
| Comparison | "5 reasons why" table · graph comparison · X for X ("Duolingo but for history") · Venn diagram · tier list |
| Call-out | Demographic call-out · "X signs you…" · "don't be an idiot" · "avoid this" · "the truth" · censored |
| Other | Starter pack · meme (cheapest CPMs, only where there are inside jokes) · quiz · recipe · song playlist · free ticket · notification offer · text on skin · side effect · "our mistake" copy · partnership carousel · green screen · sourcing |

## 4. Match format to buyer and price (@growwithevelyn)
| Audience / price | Run |
|---|---|
| Older buyers | Text-heavy ads, native-looking images (they distrust "ads") |
| Millennials | Direct response, call-out, offer ads |
| Gen Z | Educational, UGC, memes (they dislike being sold to) |
| Low ticket | Image ads (impulse) |
| High ticket | Video ads (trust) |

## 6. Testing doctrine (@raginirunsgrowth)
1. Research first: pull competitors' long-running ads (Meta Ad Library; long-running = probably profitable) and study the scripts.
2. Judge by revenue, not views. "2M views and zero conversions is trash."
3. A winner is 20% of the work. The other 80%: work out *why* it won and make variations of that concept.

## 7. Targeting and local tactics (India)
- Income proxy: run affluent neighbourhoods as separate ad sets (Delhi: Vasant Vihar, Greater Kailash, Defence Colony, Chanakyapuri,
  Golf Links / Jor Bagh) (@officialskytalks).
- Beyond Meta/Google: screens in apartment lifts and lobbies (DOOH) for premium residential targeting (@pixelspotofficial).
- Offline → online: a flyer worth picking up, with a QR code and an offer (@primepulse.inc).

## 8. Leads are only as good as the follow-up (@digital_performance_marketer)
"The plot doesn't sell, the follow-up sells."
- WhatsApp within 5 minutes (brochure + location pin).
- Three calls at three times: 11 am, 2 pm, 7-9 pm.
- Retarget "opened the form but didn't submit".
- Mark real outcomes (site visits) in the CRM and optimise for Conversion Leads so Meta learns who actually picks up.

## 9. Keep the people you already paid for (@sandraarobinson; vendor claim)
In festive season traffic triples but ~2 in 100 first-time visitors buy. Before raising budget for new visitors, recover the 98:
cart reminders on WhatsApp (claimed 28% recovery). Verify numbers before quoting.

## When this fails
- Volume without different concepts = the same ad 750 times.
- "Native" formats that mislead (fake news, fake reviews) can break platform policy and trust; the zero-star and leaked-chat formats need care.
- Tier lists are one practitioner's view at one spend level; test at ours.
