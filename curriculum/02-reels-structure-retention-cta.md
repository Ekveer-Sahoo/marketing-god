## Formats that recur (pick one on purpose)
| Format | How it runs | Seen in |
|---|---|---|
| Fast list with a filling numbered overlay | Read names fast, examples flash; too fast to absorb → saves + "comment for the list" | @byjoeym, @kolton.hustles, @yesfazal |
| Tier list | Rank items S → F; every placement is a small payoff | @matthew.feng_, @admanage.ai, @partickfranco |
| Skit / dialogue (3D or animated characters) | A character has the viewer's problem; the product or lesson is the fix | @viralspymedia, @buildwithmari_, @yapsbykhushi, @dukaandidi |
| Two-person Q&A | One asks the viewer's doubts in order: does it work? how do you know? how do I do it? | @creedom.india, @sandraarobinson |
| Teardown | Take a famous brand's page/ad and fix it live | @andrei_breha (Glossier checkout) |
| Case study | "This café / this entrepreneur found a way to…" | @primepulse.inc, @montanez_nikoo |
| Text-only over b-roll, 8-12 s | Dense text forces pause and replays; caption carries the lesson | @officialskytalks, @varunkandula_, @adithya.reddy.k |
| Daily series | "Day 22: …" builds habit and a reason to follow | @yesfazal, @gigi.yaps |
| Process → reveal (physical products) | Machine running, one surprising claim as text, hands holding result | @glint.works, @cintia.maestrello, @boundstarlight |
| Self-proving ad | The ad does to the viewer what it claims | @nothingdesign.or ("you just watched a pencil for 8 seconds") |

## Story structures that hold watch time (@learnwithraghavarora)
1. Character wants a goal → problem → failed attempts → *solution*.
2. Results first → starting point → learnings.
3. Relatable moment → conflict → realization → *the action taken*.
4. Tease the transformation → before state → *after state*.
5. Big dream → when you got serious → where you are now → *follow along*.

From the hook skit (@viralspymedia): start in the middle (day four, one shoe on, running), then go back; end where you began so it loops.

Product-story skeleton (@dankairepos): enemy (a subscription) → someone builds the alternative → proof (stars, points) → open loop → payoff.

## Retention devices
- Promise of escalation: "…but it gets progressively more niche."
- Open loop stated early, closed late ("the strangest part is…").
- Unresolved tension: cut one frame before impact.
- Numbered list on screen that fills as you go (people wait for the list to complete).
- Pattern break every 2-3 s: b-roll change, zoom, caption colour.
- Captions word by word, one keyword highlighted per line.
- Demonstrate each point as you name it.
- Short reel + long caption: the video loops while they read.

## CTAs
**One reel, one action, matched to the goal** (@aashrayy0's prompt):
| Goal | CTA |
|---|---|
| Followers | A compelling reason to follow (what's coming next) |
| Comments | An easy but meaningful question, or a keyword for a freebie |
| Saves | Make it worth revisiting (list, checklist, settings) |
| Shares | Say who should receive it ("send this to the friend whose ads get clicks but no sales") |
| Leads / sales | A natural next step, not a desperate one |

**The comment-keyword CTA** ("Comment HOOK and I'll DM you 100+ hooks") appears in over half the marketing reels in this set. It works because
a comment is the cheapest action, it boosts the reel, and the DM opens a private channel. What makes it pull:
- The freebie is the natural next step of the reel (the full list, the prompt, the template), not a generic "guide".
- The reel deliberately shows more than you can absorb.
- One keyword, said out loud and shown on screen. (@marketingwithnevaehh said "tools" while the caption said "AD": sloppy.)
- A dated event adds urgency ("workshop on Tuesday, November 17th").

**Button / on-page CTA wording** (@feras_khouri): first person, names the value or the next step.
| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| Shop now, Buy now | Find my size · Shop bestsellers · Build my bundle |
| Learn more, See more | See how it works · Take the quiz |
| Submit, Get started | Send my code · Get my 20% off |
| Shop the sale | Claim mine before it's gone · Get 40% off before midnight |
| Check it out | Finish my order |

## Content has jobs: plan the mix
| Job | Content that does it |
|---|---|
| Reach / views | Trends, tests and experiments, before/after, entertainment |
| Followers / trust | Founder day-in-the-life, stories and failures, factory visits, building in public, behind the scenes |
| Leads / sales | Testimonials, case studies, product demos, FAQ and objection posts, price breakdown ("why it's worth it"), limited drops, try-ons, offer breakdowns |

Reach-only content (festival greetings, memes, trending audio with no message) does not bring leads (@hartimesocial). Tag every idea with
its job before making it (@mohitudasi.official).

## Distribution basics (Instagram, late 2026)
- Reach recipe (@socialsoravjain): hook · keyword (say it, show it, caption it: "one right keyword beats 30 hashtags") · send-worthy ("would
  someone send this to a friend?": sends matter most for reaching non-followers) · Trial reel toggle · reply to every comment.
- Account settings (@martinwang.mp4): account status all green; website embeds on; upload at highest quality; "show account suggestions on
  profiles" on (desktop only). Settings don't make a bad video work; they stop a good one being held back.
- Profile (@marketedbyjaden): clean headshot → name = name + niche → bio = who you help + proof + CTA → highlights = wins, testimonials,
  offer → pins = offer, case study, best *converting* post.
- Volume: post far more than feels reasonable and judge total views, not single posts (@a_living_mango, @scalingsocialbrands).
- Look bigger than you are (@potatomarketing.in): many small creators post the same week, staggered by day, with one product, one problem,
  one solution, one USP and the same hashtags.
- Organic is the test bed; paid scales what already converted (@cierra.stacks).
- Music by mood (@devtalksbusiness, @maxcreates.mp4): keep a current list per mood (educational, motivational, storytelling); re-check monthly.

## Launch video checklist (@byryangambrell, from 1,700+ startup launches)
Founder's face in the first 5 s · a real number in line one · an enemy · names a new category and kills the old one · one product, one
outcome, one promise · clear within 30 s · no logo animation · vertical first · story, not demo · someone hates it · proof on screen · a
reason to act within 24 h · posted Tue/Wed · 50+ people ready to reply in the first 30 min · everywhere at once · launched repeatedly ·
measured by saves and bookmarks.

## Copy rule that travels: 10-3-1 (@laraacostar)
Max 10 words per line · emotional words in threes ("stressed, broken and burnt out") · end on 1 punchy line people quote back.

## When this fails
- Borrowed formats copied without a real freebie or insight (lots of "S-tier ads" clones; the low ones have nothing new).
- Comment-bait with a weak or unrelated freebie → comments but no customers.
- Whisper note: our transcripts of Hindi reels dropped whole 30 s windows; on-screen text was the reliable source.
