# Round 2: running list of new lessons

Collected batch by batch while studying the topic and hashtag videos. Codes point to entries in `reels-ledger.md` (batches 10-12) and
`reels-ledger-round2.md` (batch A onward). These get folded into the numbered topic files once the round is finished.
Claims from creators about platforms and statistics are unverified.

## Batches 10-12 (top reels from round-1 creators)
- Sharper proof beats bigger words: replace "premium / fast / trusted / durable" with the hours saved, who trusted it, what it survived (DZ95irCT4Bn).
- Labor illusion: showing the work raises perceived value; ask the viewer "worth it, or make it yourself?" and let them judge (Df0h4zPx367).
- Contrast: treated beside untreated in one frame beats a plain before/after (DfYCgYWM9AS).
- Story beats joined by "but" and "therefore", never "and then" (DfwMaaXhq39).
- A video is a message plus a format; collect formats separately from messages (DdakHWSg2vJ).
- Metric → cause: no views = hook; no likes = relevance; no comments = curiosity; no shares = relatability; no saves = value; no followers = profile (Df10GneFdYZ).
- Track when customers leave before guessing why; rotate what repeat buyers get (DfZFM3Bi1GX). Measure before making a rule (Dfi56URi7KU).
- Opportunity cost for small sellers: being fast at a low-value job isn't a reason to sell it (DfmcBbgqvgj).
- Name the unnamed problem and let the word carry the campaign (Dak-yeiK639).
- A second option changes the question from "buy or not" to "which one" (DHI37tivWZn). Separate brands per price point protect the core brand (DfXTf1VT2Tx).
- Stamp cards work through visible progress toward a goal (DHDsbD9ztaI).
- Unit economics per channel (LTV:CAC) decide where to focus (DXJzl3jySxZ). Career tiers keep trained staff (Ddii_P4BHns).
- Formats: live audience Q&A; "I reverse engineered [famous app]" with an oddly specific number; confession opener; "episode N" series.

## Batch A (hashtag videos, 70 studied, 8 skipped)
- Scaling: don't double an ad budget; raise about 20% every three days (worked example: doubling took cost per order from ₹333 to ₹571) (DehFRsUzla-).
- Testing matrix: persona × angle × format × awareness stage (Df66r7OtiNL).
- Small budget: 3-5 ads per ad set, test one angle, vary only the first three seconds (Df9jWclt--z).
- Promotions in three segments: competitor's buyers, non-buyers, existing customers (DfmpYI9vHzJ).
- Shrink the gap between "yes" and "paid": every extra step gives doubt time to return (DepFsBrhFiw).
- Labor illusion again, with the caveat that it backfires when the result is poor (DfSbF3njB1z).
- Three confirmation texts cut appointment cancellations (DgJMqjhPlEk).
- Decoy pricing: place a middle tier near the one you want to sell (DYUic8NqA9D).
- Get the product into the customer's hands: ownership effect (DepGBiKoDJy).
- Product story as ethos / pathos / logos (DPoLcO3Avku).
- Oddly specific numbers; pay off early, then withhold the proof; let the price do the bragging (DeAWx6Ag_tY).
- One ad, one job: a pain, a small proof, a single CTA (DcEiLTox-HD).
- Whitespace signals premium; a pick-one A/B quiz format drove 428k likes (DFGsQiOI_Us).
- Patterns: near-duplicate "native ad format" lists; comment-keyword CTAs with very high comment counts; "why [big brand] does X" psychology
  case studies in numbered series; "genius vs dumb" and "10/10" lists; Hinglish explainers with worked rupee numbers.
- Worth a closer look: DFGsQiOI_Us, DZSFkNppVW_ (a Claude ads-skill workflow), DehFRsUzla-, DepFsBrhFiw, DfDgu1Ry-Gb.
