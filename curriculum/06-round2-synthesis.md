# 06 · Round 2 synthesis

What 1,016 more videos and posts added to the first 122. Sources: `reels-ledger.md` (batches 10-12), `reels-ledger-round2.md`
(batches A-H), `posts-ledger.md`, with the per-batch lists in `round2-lessons.md`. Codes in brackets point to ledger entries.

**How to read this.** These are creators' claims, collected and de-duplicated. Nothing here has been tested by us, and numbers about
platforms, benchmarks and studies are unverified unless marked otherwise. About a quarter of the items taught nothing, and a large share
of the rest repeated each other; what follows is what was left after removing the repeats.

## 1. Hooks
- **Sharper proof, not bigger words.** Replace "premium / fast / trusted / durable" with the hours it saves, who trusted it, what it
  survived [DZ95irCT4Bn].
- **Information gap.** State what the viewer already believes, say "but", then give what they don't know. Curiosity needs partial
  knowledge [Deb7hcGMBnZ].
- **Unfinished action.** Open on something unresolved (a lit fuse) and pitch before resolving it, or borrow a suspense clip as the first
  beat [DdRbpAFNzPb, DfkdfanO2S7].
- **The first frame matters more than the wording.** The same hook got 522 views on a blank card and 35,000 typed live; test the opening
  visual before rewriting the line [Df2CsS-gdYn].
- **Delivery.** Read the hook three ways and decide the pause, the stressed word and the energy of the first second before filming
  [DcrgFS9O6_7].
- **Re-hook.** After the hook, ask a second question or add a re-hook; don't answer at once. End when the payoff lands
  [DeXnbhxurWk, P:DeQp7NPE63j].
- **A hook also repels.** Its second job is to turn away non-buyers; the words do most of the targeting [Dfa_Zd2A2Ec].
- **Four ad hook shells:** result first · the mistake · old belief vs new belief · one concrete detail [Df4q0b3vSe3].
- **Pick-one quiz** ("which looks more luxury?") drew 428k likes [DFGsQiOI_Us]. **Confession opener** drew three times more comments than
  likes [CpgURUnrKyx]. **"I reverse engineered [famous app]"** with an oddly specific number [DgE6f2khVDz].
- **Hook test:** "why would someone need to watch the next five seconds?" Attention gets the view, relevance gets the watch time
  [P:DcX5GK2ASSS].

## 2. Scripts, stories and formats
- **But / therefore, never "and then"** between story beats [DfwMaaXhq39].
- **Script template:** hook → a two- or three-sentence lock-in → body blocks, each followed by a re-hook → CTA matched to the freebie
  [DcqiQesO_R9]. **Story loop:** stakes, a big question, a twist, a re-hook [DfK4mPfNNPC].
- **A video is a message plus a format.** Collect formats separately from messages [DdakHWSg2vJ]; wrap one idea in several formats
  (myth-bust, POV, tier list, street interview, skit) [Dfrr4teP_82]; combine two proven formats into one [DdTnIjuygFF].
- **Show the work (labor illusion).** Visible effort raises perceived value, and asking "worth it, or make it yourself?" lets the viewer
  judge. It backfires when the result is poor [Df0h4zPx367, DfSbF3njB1z].
- **Side by side beats before/after** [DfYCgYWM9AS]. **Make the intangible visible** (UV damage shown as sunburn on a car) [DbGIXhHBEhV].
- **Match the demonstration to the claim:** transformation → before/after; durability → try to break it; affordability → compare with
  something costly; convenience → show the ease [Df1LbO1zX2A].
- **Decision-led founder content:** show the choice, the reasoning and the result; struggle stories are overused [DXcZdKjEuns].
- **UGC ad in four beats:** hook 0-3 s (not ad-like) · problem 3-8 s (customer's words) · proof 8-20 s · push in the last 5 s
  [P:Df2GGZonBSb]. Open with the reaction you want others to have [DZH29Ykspef].
- **AI-made UGC:** quality hinges on the first frame (a realistic phone-style photo, then animate); realism comes from deciding the
  character's situation, not from asking for "realistic" [DdruV-mhkRp, P:Dct6GqRFAFh].
- **Edits:** one- to three-word captions, cut the pauses, proof visible behind you [DfYrCNBkaMu]. Write, cut a third, cut another third
  [DLi_8kvNLwC].
- **Metric → cause:** no views = hook · no likes = relevance · no comments = curiosity · no shares = relatability · no saves = value ·
  no followers = profile [Df10GneFdYZ].
- **6 × 5 planning:** six subtopics × five formats = a month of posts [DXkEIQSjmUF]. Tag posts by funnel stage and fill the missing one
  [P:DdRem75EwPu].

## 3. Paid ads
- **What an angle is.** A reason to buy, aimed at one person at one awareness stage. A new hook or edit on the same pitch is iteration,
  not a new angle [DfXrim6xnyH].
- **Iterate a winner top-down:** avatar, angle, desire, awareness stage, mechanism, promise, offer first; hook, format, creator, pacing
  and CTA after [DegNvePzO0Q].
- **Testing matrix:** persona × angle × format × awareness stage [Df66r7OtiNL]. **Driver / Trigger / Barrier:** why they buy, when the
  problem hits, what stops them [P:DfgiBcmAXSg]. **Belief:** what they believe now → the reframe → what they should believe
  [DfAH9zEMy4K].
- **Volume by arithmetic:** winners needed = daily spend ÷ spend one winner holds; creatives to test = winners ÷ hit rate
  (₹5,000 ÷ ₹1,000 = 5; 5 ÷ 10% = 50) [DdO8zdJTTLq].
- **Small budget:** 3-5 ads per ad set, one angle, vary only the first three seconds [Df9jWclt--z]; lock the proven body and swap only the
  opening to test about 20 hooks [P:DdW-ft4jVJF]. Don't put 30 ads in one ad set [Df5WjOEvT5l].
- **Scaling:** raise budget about 20% every three days; doubling took one example from ₹333 to ₹571 per order [DehFRsUzla-].
- **Cost per purchase = CPM ÷ (1,000 × CTR × conversion rate)** [P:DfXlaSZH-rB].
- **Diagnose by symptom:** high CPM → audience or creative · low CTR → hook · high CPC → costly clicks · clicks without sales → page or
  funnel [P:DdL38iGEoB5]. Before raising budget check tracking, ad-to-page match, mobile speed, objective, surprise shipping and trust
  signals [P:Df0-AD_DD6q].
- **Judge by contribution margin:** walk one order through product cost, shipping/COD/returns and ad cost; break-even ROAS matters more
  than ROAS [Debvus9zbx3].
- **Format by funnel stage:** top = founder, UGC, native statics, POV skits · middle = comment replies, testimonials · bottom = offer and
  product statics, deadlines [P:DfoQWq8Aaku, Df7UW-nh64N].
- **Capture vs create:** search catches existing demand, social creates it. Panic test: emergency jobs are searched (start with search
  ads); planned purchases can be shown (start with social) [Df5CVQeMlHw, DfNtEiauo3k].
- **Search ads basics:** conversion tracking first; negative keywords for job seekers and price-checkers; read the search-terms report;
  buying-intent keywords; landing page with no navigation and the keyword matched [DcbuWxvgOWJ, DeRx-FtAhRZ, DfilAGABc1W, DIcQjwfS_Uc].
- **Free competitor research:** Google's Ads Transparency Center and Auction Insights; rivals' reviews for complaints to advertise
  against [DHGcsMZpPB-, DZkLffeRldW].
- **Low-quality leads usually come from setup** (volume goal, easy forms, wrong offer) [P:De8e1pSATJc].
- **Promotions in three segments:** competitors' buyers, non-buyers, existing customers [DfmpYI9vHzJ].

## 4. Offer and pricing
- **Three tiers, premium first**; the question changes from "should I?" to "which one?" [P:DfB4oR8ICmW, De3FPXATpIV]. **One extreme
  option** as an anchor [Dem1FY6vr5Z]. **Decoy caution:** reportedly held in 11 of 91 replications, so A/B your tiers [Dfw1o_cHHUS].
- **Van Westendorp price test:** too cheap to trust · a bargain · getting expensive · too expensive; price where the answers overlap
  [DE-RABNRtfB].
- **Trade every discount** for a longer term, more volume, a review or an introduction [DfgYbAtjf1t]. **Honest sales** say why it's
  cheaper [P:DfhUyZqlCIM]. **"First 100 people"** over a 72-hour sale [DaHy4xbvwED].
- **Inoculation:** name the cheaper rival's weakness before the buyer meets it [Dfo3IBkNHky].
- **Price writing:** rupees off above ₹100, percent below [Dfgt3WmiNj5]; drop the comma and ".00" [DekJrjRIp7d]; old price left, sale
  price right [DfIUVeKI3wz]; 9-endings for practical buys, round numbers for gifts [DfU8pgogZbP].
- **No hidden second bill:** fold delivery and extras into one price [DfcbQtVSNqa].
- **Word swaps:** premium not expensive, free bonus not discount, agreement not contract [P:DfUJfnpn0a2, P:DdduX7MkTKX].
- **Trial → upgrade → repeat** for products people rebuy [DgAMb4DTdI5]. **Raise order value before peak season** rather than buying more
  traffic [P:DeglTCAD7KI]. **Match funnel to price:** buy-now for low prices, a call for high ones [P:Ddnd1LtE1gN].
- **Three customer problems:** practical, emotional, and the belief underneath; solving all three takes price out of the conversation
  [P:DZ27R2liPYU].
- **Simulate 100 buyers** with an AI, each with a budget and a reason to refuse, to surface objections before launch (accuracy unverified)
  [Dfrymp_q6Rr].

## 5. Conversion and retention
- **Shrink the gap between "yes" and "paid"** [DepFsBrhFiw]. **Audit your own replies** as a customer before raising ad spend
  [DeteFTYokqJ].
- **Funnel diagnosis:** low interest → marketing · many enquiries, few purchases → offer · no repeats → product [DdyKxQvKYsM].
  **Views-to-sales math:** fix click rate before chasing views [P:DeQF5cCjRmi].
- **Landing page order:** result without the feared thing, proof, problem and its cost, solution, features with benefits, steps, FAQ, CTA
  [Dcly790oumZ]. By stage: advertorial or quiz at the top, comparison and reviews in the middle, bundle builder or booking at the bottom
  [Df-MoqqxEWX]. Whitespace reads as premium [DFGsQiOI_Us].
- **Popups:** a quiz or game instead of "10% off", with the code auto-applied [DfFmWaAN6xT].
- **After the sale:** narrate the order by email, then a usage sequence with 30/60/90-day expectations [DfsVUzls_x_]; three
  confirmation texts cut cancellations [DgJMqjhPlEk].
- **Track when customers leave before guessing why;** rotate what repeat buyers get [DfZFM3Bi1GX]. **Measure before making a rule**
  [Dfi56URi7KU]. An unrequested extra keeps customers (₹30 to retain vs ₹400 to acquire) [Den3QkrTJnY].
- **Follow-up:** after "we'll get back to you", ask whether they lean yes or no [DWiFv5-k7DO].

## 6. Brand and campaigns
- **Name the unnamed problem** and let the word carry the campaign [Dak-yeiK639, DbagmfuIKyH]. **Enemy:** position against what the
  customer hates [DSmojfXjSbF]. **State a limit as the claim** ("we don't do frozen") [DdxYp6zCKGe].
- **Let customers be the proof** (a hidden-tag contest; bottles printed with each restaurant's name) [DbYMG-Nsrbs, DZH9LqouMwA].
- **Tie the number to the story** (a shirt run matching the animals left) [DaVGdnQhWVp].
- **Two or three repeatable signatures,** and say no to everything off-system [DZxkQ08Rrn1]. Don't change a recognisable asset without
  testing [DcaGAUftw5s].
- **Separate brands per price point** protect the core brand [DfXTf1VT2Tx].

## 7. What the 1,000 show about the medium itself
- **Comment-keyword gates drive comments, not quality.** The highest comment counts (thousands to tens of thousands) came from
  "comment WORD for the guide" on tool and prompt giveaways, regardless of what the post taught.
- **Repetition is the norm.** Whole hashtags are dominated by one script reposted by many accounts (ad-format lists, decoy-pricing
  explainers, one Claude ads-skill walkthrough). Novelty is rare, so a specific, sourced, original point stands out.
- **Captions rarely carry the lesson.** 168 of 445 carousel captions only teased the slides.
- **Numbers are mostly unsourced.** Treat every statistic in these reels as a hypothesis.
- **Series and case studies travel:** "Day N", "Episode N", "why [big brand] does X", fictional shopkeeper stories with worked rupee sums.
