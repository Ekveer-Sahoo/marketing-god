**DeFATG6umBR · @hormozi · 31.0k likes / 6.9k cmts · 92s** — the hook × meat × CTA ad matrix (core doctrine, see `ads-creative.md`)
An ad = hook + meat + CTA. Don't write one ad: write 50 hooks × 3-5 meats × 1-3 CTAs = 150-750 ads/week ("huge for Andromeda: variety").
Step 1, 50 hooks from five sources: your own past winning ad hooks; hooks from your free content; other people's winning ad hooks; other
people's winning free content; platform ad libraries. Spread hooks across awareness levels (or one level if that's who you target).
Step 2, 3-5 meats (educate on the offer/product/solution/problem). Five formats: demonstration, testimonial, educational, story, faceless.
Step 3, 1-3 CTAs: show and tell exactly what to do next. Step 4, film everything in one session, edit combinations.
Priority (caption): #1 get attention (visual or verbal hook), #2 tell them what to do (CTA), #3 why (everything else).
Fmt: "Here's how I took a company's ads from this to this" (proof contrast) → numbered steps → comment MAP.
