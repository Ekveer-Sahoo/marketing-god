# Reel 01: "This isn't a sticker. Listen."

Product: custom UV-texture magnetic card wallet · Goal: DM orders · Format: Instagram Reel, 9:16, about 18 s
Built with `skills/hooks` and `skills/video-ads`. Research behind it is a draft on assumptions (`magsafe-wallet-audience.md`).

## Hook
| Layer | |
|---|---|
| Visual | Extreme close-up: a fingernail drags slowly across the raised print, light raking from the side so the ridges throw shadows |
| Text on screen | **This isn't a sticker.** |
| Spoken / sound | The scratchy sound of the nail on the ridges, then: "This isn't a sticker. Listen." |

Type: material reveal (show-it). Score: Specific 2 · Viewer 2 · Loop 1 · Clear 2 · Muted 2 · Unswappable 2 = **11/12**.
Why it should work: movement and an odd texture in frame one, a sound people want to hear again, and it answers the first doubt
("it's just a printed sticker") before it's raised. It needs sound on to land fully; the on-screen text carries it muted.

Every beat is `film` because every beat is proof: the texture, the process and the hold are exactly what the viewer is being asked to
believe. Nothing here should be AI-generated.

## Shot list (about 20 minutes, phone, one lamp)
Set the phone on a stand, lamp low and to one side, dark plain surface. Shoot each three times.
1. Macro: nail across the print (get as close as the phone will focus; record the sound in a quiet room).
2. Macro: tilt the wallet under the lamp; thumb rub.
3. Printer: head moving over the wallet, 5 s; then hand lifting the finished piece.
4. Three designs fanned; one snapping onto a phone (record the click).
5. Shake test with cards in.
6. Hold the phone to camera, say the CTA line.
Voiceover: record the four lines separately, close to the mic.

## Edit
Cuts every 2-3 s · captions word by word, one highlighted word per line (bold above) · no music under beat 1 so the nail sound is clean,
music in from beat 3 · caption text: what it is, price, lead time, "custom pieces can't be returned", the keyword again.
