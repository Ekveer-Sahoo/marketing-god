# Marketing plan: custom UV-texture magnetic card wallets

Date: 2026-11-25 · Status: first draft on assumptions (see `magsafe-wallet-audience.md`). Prices and numbers marked "to test" are
starting points, not findings.

## 1. What we're selling, in one line
A magnetic card wallet with **your own design printed in raised texture you can feel**, made one piece at a time.

Why this can stand out: plain magnetic wallets are a commodity sold on price. A flat custom print is common. A **textured** print that
catches light and that a fingernail can feel is something people show each other, and it films well. That texture is the product's
"purple cow"; everything in the plan points at it.

**Wording:** "MagSafe" is Apple's trademark. Say "MagSafe-compatible" or "magnetic wallet for iPhone 12 and newer", not "MagSafe wallet".
**Designs:** don't print characters, team crests, brand logos or celebrity photos you don't have rights to. Customers' own photos, names,
pets, original art and their own business logos are fine.

## 2. Who first
1. **Phone personalisers, 18-30, iPhone users in India**, buying for themselves on Instagram.
2. **Gift buyers** (partner, best friend, sibling): name, photo, pet or couple design.
3. Later: **bulk** for college fests, clubs and small brands (needs one finished batch and a bulk price first).

## 3. Before any marketing: five things to make real
These are the proof. Without them the content has nothing true to show.
1. **Six sample wallets** in different styles (name in bold type, pet portrait, pattern, a map or skyline, a line-art face, a small-brand logo).
2. **A scratch-and-rub test** and a **shake test**, done honestly. If either fails, fix the product before promoting it.
3. **Fixed facts**: price, card capacity, lead time (dispatch vs delivery), payment options, which phones and cases it holds on, no-return
   policy for custom pieces.
4. **A simple order flow**: DM a keyword → they send the image or name → you send a digital preview → they pay → you print.
5. **Real macro footage** of the printer laying down the texture, and of light raking across a finished piece.

## 4. Offer
- **Price (to test):** somewhere in the ₹699-999 range for one custom wallet; test two prices with different enquirers for a week.
  Work out your floor first: blank + ink + packaging + shipping + your time.
- **Free shipping** folded into the price, not added at checkout.
- **A win, not a discount:** hold the price and add a small bonus (their name on the inside edge, a matching textured keychain or sticker).
- **Bundles:** two wallets for a couple or best friends at a pair price; wallet + matching keychain.
- **Free digital preview before they pay.** It removes the biggest custom-order fear and gives them something to share.
- **Real limits only:** "I print 10 a day" or an order-by date for a festival, if true.

## 5. Channels
| Channel | Job | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | Reach and proof | The main channel. Texture and process footage. |
| Instagram DM (keyword) | Sales | "Comment or DM DESIGN and I'll send you a preview." Reply to every comment. |
| WhatsApp | Closing, repeat and bulk orders | Catalogue of sample designs; broadcast to past buyers before gifting occasions. |
| Customer posts | Free reach | Every design looks different, so each buyer has a reason to post theirs. Ask for the tag; repost with permission. |
| Small creators (10-20) | Looks-bigger-than-you-are week | Send personalised pieces; ask them to post in the same week, staggered by day, same hashtag. |
| Paid ads | Scale only what already worked | Start after one reel has clearly outperformed. Small daily budget, message objective, 4-6 different creatives. |
| Marketplaces / Etsy | Later | Custom orders are harder there; consider once the design flow is smooth. |

## 6. Content plan: first two weeks (9 reels)
Each one is tagged with its job. Hooks follow `skills/hooks`; sales reels end on the DM keyword.

| # | Reel | Job | Hook (draft) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nail across the texture | Sales | "This isn't a sticker. Listen." |
| 2 | Blank in, design out | Reach | "Blank in. Your design out." |
| 3 | Send me a photo | Sales | "Send me your dog's photo. This is what comes back." |
| 4 | Scratch, rub, shake test | Trust | "Three ways I tried to ruin this print." (only after the tests pass) |
| 5 | How many cards, will it fall | Trust | "Will it fall off? Watch." |
| 6 | Six designs, pick one | Reach + research | "Which one would you carry? 1 to 6." |
| 7 | Price said out loud | Sales | "₹[price] for a card holder? Watch what goes into it." |
| 8 | A real customer's piece and reaction | Trust | Their words, with permission |
| 9 | Gift version | Sales | "Their name. In texture. Under ₹[price]." |

Reply to every comment and DM. Tally what people ask; those questions become the next reels and update the audience file.

## 7. Numbers to watch
- **Per reel:** 3-second hold, saves, shares, DMs started.
- **Sales:** enquiries → previews sent → paid orders; which reel each buyer came from (ask them).
- **Break-even:** fixed costs this month ÷ profit per wallet = wallets to sell. Fill this in once the price and costs are set.
- **Judge by orders, not views.**

| Week | Do |
