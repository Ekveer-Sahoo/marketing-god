# Video recipes

Named recipes so a plan can say "use Anticipation cut for the hook" and everyone knows what that means. Each lists what's real and what may be
generated. Sources: reels in `curriculum/reels-ledger.md`. Untested in the lab; treat performance claims as hypotheses.

## Structure recipes (the whole video)

| Recipe | Beats | Needs | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Transformation** | Before state → the change happening → after state → what it is → CTA | Real footage or photos of before and after | Anything where the product visibly changes something |
| **Anticipation cut** | Your own clip started two seconds before the outcome → outcome → what you just saw → offer → CTA | A real clip with a moment of suspense (first take-off, a pour, a reveal) | Events, services, making-of |
| **Raw vs finished** | Raw piece dropped or held → hard cut to the finished piece → a physical test (tap, weight) → maker on camera, one line on how it's made → what you get → CTA | The raw and the finished item, the maker for one line; **no process footage needed** | Made things when the process can't be filmed |
| **Price objection** | The price said as a doubt → "watch what it goes through" → process fast → result → CTA | Process footage | Handmade, made-to-order, higher-priced items |
| **Question → answer skit** | Buyer asks the real questions in order (does it work? how do you know? how do I get it?) → short answers over proof | Two voices or text bubbles; proof clips | Considered purchases, parents, B2B |
| **Ad break** | Suspense opener cut before its payoff → "ad break, 10, 9, 8…" → pitch → return to the payoff | An opener with a real payoff (your own clip, or a generated one that isn't presented as real) | Entertainment-led reach with a pitch inside |
| **Story compare** | Two people, the better product loses → why → the fix → moral in one line | Illustration or animation (can be generated) | Teaching a principle; service sellers |
| **Self-proving** | The video does to the viewer what it claims, then says so | A simple, tense visual | Creative, design and attention services |
| **Tier list / fast list** | Items ranked or listed fast with a filling on-screen list → CTA for the full list | Examples to flash; graphics | Education, lead magnets |
| **Founder story** | Objection people said → the start, including a failure → why others fail → what's offered → CTA | Founder on camera in the real workplace | Training, workshops, personal brands |

## Shot recipes (one generated or filmed beat)

| Recipe | What it is | Real input | Generated? | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Photo-to-flythrough reveal** | Camera flies through the city or street and arrives at the real shop | A clear photo of the storefront | Yes, image-to-video from the photo | Signage text warping; the shop must stay recognisable |
| **Assemble-from-pieces** | The shop or product builds itself from parts | Photo of the real shop or product | Yes | Final frame must match the real thing |
| **Product hero in scene** | The real product in a setting you can't shoot (studio light, a lifestyle scene) | Clean product photo on plain background | Yes | Shape, colour, logo and finish drift; reject any take that changes the product |
| **Process close-up** | Hands or machine doing the real work | — | **No. Film it**; this is proof | Good light, steady phone, 3-5 seconds per action |
| **Talking object** | The product or an ingredient "speaks" a line | Product photo | Yes (clearly stylised) | Keep it obviously playful, not a fake person |
| **Recurring character skit** | A consistent animated character plays the buyer or the helper | A character sheet made once | Yes | Keep the character identical across clips; don't imitate known characters |
| **Faceless explainer** | Narration over simple animated scenes with captions | Script | Yes | No talking heads; one voice, one style throughout |
| **Creator-style review** | A person talks to camera about the product | The product; the script | Only as clearly AI or with a real consenting creator; never as a "customer" | This is the easiest place to fabricate proof. Don't. |
| **Split screen** | Product or screen on top, face below | Screen recording or product footage + phone video | No | Keep captions out of the seam |
| **Suspense opener** | A short, tense, obviously staged clip that ends before the outcome | Idea only | Yes | Must pay off later in the video; not presented as a real event |
| **Text-over-b-roll** | 8-12 seconds of calm footage with dense on-screen text | Any relevant b-roll | Either | Readable in the time given; the caption carries the detail |

## Finishing checklist
- 9:16, key content clear of the top and bottom interface zones.
- Something moves in frame one; on-screen hook text appears within the first half second.
- Captions word by word, one highlighted keyword per line, added in the edit.
- A cut or visible change every 2-3 seconds.
- One CTA, spoken and shown, matching the destination.
- Sound: music chosen for the mood (educational, motivational, storytelling); the voice clearly above it.
- Real proof is real; generated parts are labelled where the platform requires.

## More recipes from round 2
Added from the round 2 study (`curriculum/06-round2-synthesis.md`). Untested in the lab.

| Recipe | Beats | Needs | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Side by side** | Treated and untreated in one frame from the first second → how → result → CTA | Both states filmed together | Cleaning, repair, finishes, anything with a visible difference |
| **Show the work** | "Is this worth ₹X, or would you make it yourself?" → the real process, unhurried → the result → CTA | Real process footage | Handmade and made-to-order; backfires if the result is ordinary |
| **UGC four beats** | Hook 0-3 s (doesn't look like an ad) → problem 3-8 s in the customer's words → proof 8-20 s → push in the last 5 s (one reason to act now) | A real customer or a consenting creator | Paid social for consumer products |
| **Hook → lock-in → blocks** | Hook → two or three sentences that say why to stay → body blocks, each ending on a re-hook → CTA matched to what's offered | A script | Talking-head explainers |
| **Make it visible** | A visible stand-in for an invisible benefit or harm → the product as the fix | One strong visual idea | Protection, hygiene, safety, anything unseen |

**Script check.** Read the beats aloud joined by "and then". Wherever that works, the story is flat; rewrite so the beats join with "but"
or "therefore".

**AI-made creator-style video.** Realism comes from the first frame and the situation, not from the word "realistic": start from a
phone-style still, and decide the character's circumstances (late, tired, halfway through a routine). The rule against generated
customers and testimonials still applies; this is for clearly presented or consenting creators only.

**Edit.** Captions of one to three words, pauses cut, proof visible behind the speaker.
