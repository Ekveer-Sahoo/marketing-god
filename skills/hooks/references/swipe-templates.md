# Swipe templates

Fill the blanks with the audience's own words and one specific detail. A template is a starting shape, not a finished hook: score it
after filling. These are written for this plugin, grouped by the trigger each one pulls; the ideas behind them are common copywriting
patterns seen across many creators (see `curriculum/reels-ledger.md` for where each pattern was observed). Most suit creators, coaches
and services; for anything with something to show, start from the show-it types in `hook-types.md`.

## What separates a working hook from a dead one
| A working hook | A dead one |
|---|---|
| Says exactly what the piece is about | Could introduce any piece on any topic |
| Leaves one thing unanswered | Finishes its own thought |
| Names a result or a cost | Promises "value" or "tips" |
| Uses words the audience uses | Uses marketer words |
| Makes the next line necessary | Makes the next line optional |

Dead shapes to recognise (the binding ban list is in `scoring.md`): announcing that something is interesting, greeting the viewer,
saying you have been thinking about something, promising it "could change everything", telling everyone they need to know this.

## Curiosity and knowledge gap
1. The reason your ___ isn't working has nothing to do with ___.
2. Nobody mentions this part of ___ until you've already paid for it.
3. Two people do the same ___. One gets ___. Here's the difference.
4. I changed one thing about my ___ and ___ happened.
5. There's a ___ setting almost nobody turns on.
6. What ___ actually looks like after ___ months.

## Problem and frustration
7. Tried everything for ___ and still stuck at ___?
8. If your ___ keeps ___, check this first.
9. You're doing ___ right. It's the ___ that's letting you down.
10. This is why ___ feels harder than it should.

## Loss and stakes
11. Before you spend another ₹___ on ___, look at this.
12. Ignoring ___ is costing you about ___ a month.
13. Fix this before your next ___, or ___.
14. Every week you leave ___ as it is, you lose ___.

## Desire and outcome
15. Picture ___ without having to ___.
16. How to get ___ in ___ days when you only have ___.
17. The easiest way I know to go from ___ to ___.
18. What changes when you stop ___ and start ___.

## Proof and specificity
19. We took ___ from ___ to ___. Here's each step.
20. ___ orders in ___ days from one ___.
21. ___ years of doing ___ taught me this about ___.
22. I tested ___ against ___ for ___ days. The result surprised me.

## Belief shift
23. You don't need more ___. You need a better ___.
24. ___ isn't a ___ problem. It's a ___ problem.
25. Everyone says ___. I think that's backwards, and here's why.
26. Not everyone should ___. Here's who shouldn't.

## Objection and price
27. ₹___ for a ___? Fair. Watch what goes into it.
28. Is ___ worth it? Three questions to ask first.
29. The difference between buying a ___ and paying for ___.
30. Not sure ___ is for you? This will tell you in 20 seconds.

## Authority and experience
31. If I had to start ___ again from zero, I'd do these three things.
32. As a ___ for ___ years, here's what I'd never ___.
33. I wish someone had shown me this before my first ___.

## Call-out and mistake
34. If you ___ and still ___, you're probably making this mistake.
35. Stop ___ if you want ___.
36. The most common mistake I see beginners make with ___.
37. This habit is quietly wrecking your ___.

## Honest negative (needs a real insight behind it)
38. Unpopular view: ___ is overrated for most people.
39. Can we stop pretending ___ works for ___?
40. What you see online about ___ leaves out ___.
41. Just because ___ worked for them doesn't mean it fits you.
42. I might lose followers for this, but ___.

Negative hooks earn attention by challenging a belief or exposing a gap. Without a real reason behind them they read as rage-bait.
