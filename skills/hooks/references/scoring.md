# Hook scoring

Score each criterion 0, 1 or 2. Total out of 12. Keep 8+; 10+ is strong. Be harsh: compare against what a top creator would ship.

| # | Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Specific** | Adjectives and generalities | One concrete element | A number, named situation or concrete object carries the line (good footage is not enough; the *words* must be specific too) |
| 2 | **For the viewer** | About the brand, creator or news | Implies who it's for | Names the viewer or their exact situation, **or** shows the result they want actually happening (a demo or transformation counts) |
| 3 | **Opens a loop** | Nothing left to find out | Mild curiosity | A clear question they need answered, and the piece can answer it |
| 4 | **Clear in one read** | Needs rereading or context | Understandable | Lands in under two seconds, plain words |
| 5 | **Works muted / at a glance** (video: visual + text; text formats: the first 6-8 words) | Nothing happens | Text only | Movement or an odd image in frame one plus readable text |
| 6 | **Not swappable** | Any competitor could run it | Category-specific, or made local only by city / price / batch size | Rests on something only this offer has (its mechanism, a named result, its own footage or customer words) |

Criteria 2 and 6 can pull against each other; a 12 is rare and 10 is excellent.

## Fatal flags (drop or rewrite regardless of score)
- **Can't pay off**: the content doesn't deliver what the hook promises.
- **Invented proof**: a number, result or credential that isn't real.
- **Overstated product**: says or implies the product is something it isn't (e.g. "solid gold" for a gold-tone plated finish).
- **Banned opener** (the one list; always rewrite): "In this video…" · "Hello and welcome…" · "Have you ever wondered…" · "Here's something
  interesting…" · "Today I'm sharing N tips" · "Let's talk about…" · "Just wanted to share…" · "Everyone needs to know this".
- **Misleading native format**: pretends to be news, a review or a private chat in a way that would deceive, or breaks platform ad policy.

## Failure tags (use these names so mistakes can be counted in the lab)
| Tag | Meaning | Typical fix |
|---|---|---|
| `generic` | Fits any competitor | Add the one detail only this product has |
| `about-us` | Leads with brand, creator or announcement | Flip to the viewer's situation or stake |
| `no-loop` | No reason to see the next second | Hold back the outcome; promise it |
| `empty-intensifier` | "Huge", "insane", "game-changer" | Replace with the number or the thing |
| `vague-benefit` | "Grow faster", "get better results" | State the result and the time or cost |
| `too-slow` | Point arrives after second three | Start in the middle; cut the wind-up |
| `no-visual` | Talking head, static frame one | Add movement, a prop, or a proof contrast on screen |
| `overpromise` | Bigger than the content | Shrink the promise or strengthen the content |
| `wrong-audience-words` | Marketer language, not buyer language | Use a phrase from the audience research |
| `rage-bait` | Negative with no insight behind it | Add the honest reason, or drop the negativity |
