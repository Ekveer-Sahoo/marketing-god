# Ad brief: drone workshop, 13-14 December batch

- **Result wanted:** paid bookings, taken on WhatsApp. Every ad opens a WhatsApp chat.
- **Budget:** ₹500 a day for 14 days on Instagram = ₹7,000.
- **Capacity:** 8 finished ads this week (5 video, 3 image). 6 go live first, 2 are held as swaps.
- **Research status:** built on `audience.md`, which rests on 10 WhatsApp messages. Treat every choice here as provisional.
- **Tracker:** `ad-matrix.csv` in this folder. Name each ad in Instagram/Meta with its ID from that file.

## Check before anything is published
1. **13 and 14 December 2026 fall on a Sunday and a Monday.** No ad says "weekend", "Saturday" or "Sunday". Confirm the dates.
2. **Fee and seats.** ₹2,999 and 12 seats come from the earlier batch. Confirm both for this one.
3. **Parent's message.** I have assumed it is "he attended last batch and has not stopped talking about it". Confirm that is the one she allowed, and that she is fine with it in a paid ad. It runs as "parent, last batch", with no name.
4. **Children's faces.** The flying and take-off clips show other people's children. Get their parents' OK for ad use, or use shots where faces are not clear.
5. **Venue.** Not known yet. Ads say "Bhubaneswar"; give the address in the WhatsApp reply.
6. **Supervision.** No ad says anything about safety staff or ratios, because I don't know them. "No soldering, snap-fit kit" is all that is claimed.

## Setting it up (short version; Meta's screens change, so wording may differ)
1. You need an Instagram professional account, a Facebook Page linked to it, and the WhatsApp Business app on the booking number, linked to the Page.
2. In Meta Ads Manager, create one campaign whose goal lets you choose **WhatsApp** as where people message you (at the time of writing this sits under Engagement or Leads → messaging apps). Don't pick a traffic or video-views goal: those buy clicks and views, not enquiries.
3. One ad set: ₹500 a day, Bhubaneswar plus about 15-20 km, adults roughly 30-50, Instagram placements. Leave interests broad to begin with.
4. Put the six day-1 ads in that one ad set. Name each with its ID.
5. If Ads Manager lets you set the pre-filled WhatsApp message per ad, end it with the hook code, e.g. "Hi, I'd like details of the 13-14 Dec drone workshop (H01)". Then every enquiry tells you which ad sent it.
6. Have a saved WhatsApp reply ready with venue, fee, UPI details, the no-refund / move-to-next-batch rule and what to bring.

I have not checked these steps against Meta's current help pages (no web access this session).

## Test plan
- **What counts:** paid seats. WhatsApp chats started is the early signal. Three bookings bring in more than the ₹7,000 ad spend, before your kit and venue costs.
- **Days 1-4:** touch nothing (about ₹2,000 spent).
- **Day 5 rule:** pause any ad that has spent ₹400 or more with no WhatsApp chat, or that costs more than twice as much per chat as the best ad. Put the two held ads in their place. If nothing has earned a chat by then, the problem is more likely the audience setting or the offer than any one ad; tell me before changing ads.
- **Day 9-10:** same rule again. For the last four days keep the best two or three ads plus the date card.
- **Stop when the 12 seats are full.**
- **Reading a failure:** people don't watch past 3 seconds → the hook. They watch but don't message → the body or CTA. They message but don't book → the reply, the fee or the dates.
- **Log:** for each enquiry note the ad code and whether they booked. Fill `results` (bookings) and `spend` in the CSV at day 5, day 10 and the end.
- **After a winner:** compare IDs to see which part won, then put new hooks from the wave-3 list on the winning body.

These thresholds are starting guesses for a first campaign, not tested numbers.
