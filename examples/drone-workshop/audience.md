## Summary (write this last)
- **Best first segment:** A Bhubaneswar parent of a class 7-10 child who is already asking about drones or robotics, deciding over WhatsApp whether ₹2,999 for one weekend is worth it. Why first: parents pay, most of the messages read like parent questions, and the product's clearest answer (the child builds a drone, flies it and takes it home) can be shown on video. [inferred: from the price, safety, certificate and JEE messages]
- **Not for:** parents who want exam prep (JEE value is unproven); families who need a refund option; children below class 7 unless the seller decides otherwise; anyone who cannot do both weekend days.
- **Top problem:** ₹2,999 for two days feels like a lot next to a free school robotics club (2 of 10 messages touch price).
- **Lead angle:** The price doubt, answered by day 2: they fly the drone they built and it goes home with them.
