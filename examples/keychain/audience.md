# Audience: Electroplated 3D-printed custom keychains

- **Product / outcome:** 3D-printed keychain with a real electroplated metal finish (copper, nickel, gold-tone over plastic), custom name or logo, made to order. Outcome: a personalised object that looks like metal and is "theirs" (their name, their logo), mostly to give or to show.
- **Price / where sold:** ₹499 · Instagram DMs, India
- **Last updated:** 2026-11-25 · **Evidence mix:** about 60% own · 0 public · about 40% assumed (by tagged line; source = 10 DMs/comments, no search tool used)

**Draft status:** built from 10 own DMs/comments plus assumptions. No public voice-of-customer was collected (no search tool). Every count below is out of 10 messages, so ×1 means "seen once", not "a pattern".

## Best first segment
Someone aged roughly 18-25 buying a personalised gift for a girlfriend or best friend with a date coming up (birthday), ordering by DM on a budget of about ₹500. Why first: gifting is the only use that shows up more than once in the evidence (2 of 10 messages, one of them a completed order of 2 pieces that "they loved"), a deadline creates urgency, and gift value makes ₹499 easier to justify than "a keychain". [own ×2 for gifting; age and budget are assumed]
Not for: people who want a solid-metal keychain; anyone who needs it in 2 days if made-to-order lead time is longer [own ×1 asked, lead time unknown]; buyers comparing on price with cheap printed or acrylic keychains [assumed].

## Decisions
### Top three problems
| # | Problem | Hurts (1-5) | Frequency in evidence | Product fit (1-5) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Will it reach me before the date? (gift deadline, urgency) | 5 | 2 of 10 | Unknown until lead time is stated; made-to-order works against it | "gf ka bday 28 ko hai", "can I get it in 2 days, urgent" |
| 2 | Is it real and will it last? (metal vs paint, colour coming off) | 4 | 2 of 10 | 4 if plating durability holds up (not verified) | "is this real metal or just paint??", "colour utar to nahi jayega" |
| 3 | Is ₹499 worth it for a keychain, and is it safe to pay? (price, COD) | 3 | 2 of 10, or 3 counting the bare "price pls" | 3 | "499 thoda zyada nahi hai keychain ke liye?", "bro COD available?" |

Hurt and fit scores are judgement [assumed]; only the frequency column is evidence.

### Angles
1. "Real metal or just paint?" → it's actual copper/nickel plated onto the piece — believable because the plating process can be shown on camera.
2. Girlfriend's or best friend's birthday coming up → a gift with their name in metal, delivered by a stated date — believable because of a clear order-by cutoff and the buyer who ordered 2 and "they loved it".
3. "499 for a keychain?" → it's a made-to-order, plated, personalised gift, not a keychain — believable because each one is designed, printed and plated individually (show the steps).
4. Your bike's logo, in metal, on your keys → believable because of a shown logo-to-finished-piece before/after.
5. Fest or club merch that doesn't look like cheap merch → believable only once there is a bulk price and one finished batch to show. (Weakest: 1 message.)
